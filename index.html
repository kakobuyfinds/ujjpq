From a find to a decision

How to use this LitBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is a comparison tool: one row for each exact product option, with its source, price and unanswered questions. Use this selection to begin your research, then confirm order details on the platform you choose.

Shortlist the exact option

Filter the catalog, save relevant finds and open their CAFANS pages. Record the listing ID, seller, color, size and quantity. A photograph showing a full outfit does not confirm that every piece is included.

Confirm the buying route

On CAFANS, review the available buying links. If choosing LitBuy, check the destination domain and confirm that the item and selected option can be ordered. This page does not place an order or convert links.

Review before shipping

Compare order-specific warehouse photos with the option you ordered. Resolve size or condition questions within the applicable return window, then obtain a shipping quote for your parcel and destination.

Answers before you order

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize finds for potential orders through LitBuy. A useful list keeps the original product link, exact option, price reference, measurements and order notes together. This independent page supplies CAFANS product references and a downloadable starting tracker.

Is this an official LitBuy website?

No. This page is an independent product reference and is not operated by or affiliated with LitBuy. It does not accept payments, manage accounts or provide warehouse services. The two main buttons lead to CAFANS.

Does “Buy it now” place an order?

No. It opens the CAFANS website, where you can browse products and available buying links. Confirm the product, destination platform and current order terms before paying.

Are these current prices, and is shipping included?

The displayed USD amounts are a snapshot of CAFANS homepage estimates retrieved on 19 November 2026. They are not live quotes. International shipping, taxes and additional charges are not included in these reference prices. A different size, color or quantity may change the item price.

Are the products verified or authenticated?

No purchase, seller audit, authentication or physical inspection is claimed here. The product names and listing images come from CAFANS. Check material claims, measurements, seller information and order-specific photos before deciding.

Can I open the download in Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Save at least one product and download the CSV. Open it in Excel or import it into Google Sheets. It contains source links, reference USD amounts and blank columns for the exact option, sizing, current quote, QC and shipping notes.

Where is my saved shortlist stored?

Saved product IDs are stored in this browser when local storage is available. They are not synced between devices. If storage is blocked, saving works only for the current page session. The download is generated locally; this page has no sign-up form or analytics script.
